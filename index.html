Today's readings

Silver's live price here is a same-day snapshot, but its full history is now charted below alongside gold and the miners — sourced from COMEX silver futures and rescaled to match this spot quote. (An earlier version of this dashboard said no free silver history existed; that turned out to be wrong, and it has been added.) Rates and oil come from the Federal Reserve’s FRED database, which publishes a day or more behind — any reading marked as of a date is that source’s latest real print, not today’s. Oil is shown as futures prices (current to the day); the scores and backtests use the FRED spot series instead, which lags days behind, and FRED’s Brent is the physical “Dated Brent” price, which can sit far above the futures price when supply is tight. The dollar is the ICE US Dollar Index (DXY); the scores themselves use the Fed’s broad trade-weighted index, which has the long history the backtests need.

Forward test — how the three plans do from now on

Every backtest above is fitted to history. This is the real test. On the start date each plan's settings were frozen; from then on every trade a plan takes is logged exactly as it appeared, with its stop moves and exit, and never edited afterwards. After a few months this will show whether the plans behave the way the backtest says.
